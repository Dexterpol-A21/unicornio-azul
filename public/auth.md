# auth.md

Unicornio Azul does not operate an OAuth or OpenID Connect authorization server and does not register AI agents.

## Audience

Human visitors and AI agents researching business development, product, international sourcing, marketing, and digital channels.

## Registration

There is no `register_uri`, no client credentials, and no token endpoint on this origin. Do not send OAuth authorize or token requests here.
