## How to engage

There is no OAuth, no agent account, and no paid API. Do not invent credentials.

Humans (or agents acting for them) should use:

- Form: https://unicornioazul.es/empezar
- Email: luis.chicharro@unicornioazul.es

A useful brief includes: the idea or product, target market, whether there is a patent, expected volume, and whether they need factory search, import, brand, or Amazon.
